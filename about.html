About Me

Why this project exists

I made this website for my yearbook class to explore the shift from physical game media to digital distribution.

It looks at how different platforms approach physical releases, digital libraries, and the question of what it means to own a game.

Browse the platform pages to learn more, or visit the Sources page for references.
