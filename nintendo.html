NINTENDO

Nintendo's Approach to Physical Media

Unlike some of its competitors, Nintendo continues to support physical game releases while also expanding its digital offerings.

Nintendo's approach to physical media gives players the option between traditional physical game cards and digital purchases.

Physical Game Cards

Traditional physical releases store the game data directly on the game card.

Game Key Cards

Game key cards use a physical card to provide access to a game that must be downloaded.
