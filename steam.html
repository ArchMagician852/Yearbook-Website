Steam's Digital-First Approach

Steam has helped make digital game distribution the standard for PC gaming. Games can be purchased, downloaded, and accessed without ever needing a physical disc.

But when you purchase a game digitally, what exactly are you buying? The convenience of digital distribution can come with an important distinction between owning a physical copy and receiving a license to use digital software.

YOU DON'T OWN THE GAME

A physical game can exist independently of a company's servers. Digital games depend much more heavily on the platform and the licenses that allow them to be accessed.

THE CONVENIENCE

Steam makes purchasing and managing a game library incredibly convenient. No discs, no trips to a store, and no physical storage required.

THE TRADE-OFF

Digital distribution provides convenience, but it also means your access can depend on the platform, your account, and the availability of the software.
